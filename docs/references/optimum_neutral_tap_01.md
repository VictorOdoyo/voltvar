# optimum neutral tap 01

With no load or capacitor, a neutral tap produces exactly 1 pu and zero operations. Every other allowed tap has positive voltage deviation and/or switching penalty.

Inputs and expected values: [fixture](../../references/optimum_neutral_tap_01.json). Variant 1 exercises a distinct operating point or bound within this family. The regression uses 1e-7 relative and absolute tolerance unless checking solver residual explicitly. This is an analytical example, not a certified field study.
